**PostLens 2 is now available for iPhone and iPad.**

This release brings a more complete editing workspace to the same flow I started with: choose a photo, improve it, compose the result, and export something you are ready to share.

One part remains unavailable: **direct Instagram publishing**. We are waiting for Meta approval for production, so Instagram connection and publishing are excluded from the live app. You can still export your work and share it manually.

## More room to work on the photo

PostLens 2 organizes editing into focused **Filters, Tune, Crop, and Retouch** workspaces. Those steps have different jobs, and giving each one its own space makes it easier to stay with the image while moving between them.

The release adds on-device object repair and AI Color, expands filters and effects, and keeps accepted edits reversible through undo and redo. In Retouch, Remove and Heal provide focused repair tools. The original remains a useful reference while you decide whether a change improves the photo.

The library workflow also extends to Apple Photos albums, folders, and Shared Albums. Selected photos can continue into editable carousels and layouts, with caption assistance close to the creative work.

The point is to reduce the distance between finding an image worth using and finishing it.

## The private workflow stays central

Core photo analysis and editing continue to run on the device. The editing workflow remains usable without Apple Intelligence; optional Apple intelligence features depend on device support.

That distinction matters when describing the app. Normal editing, optional system intelligence, and publishing to a social platform have different requirements. They should be clear before someone chooses to use them.

Private planning is available through the optional Supporter tools. You can prepare work and use local reminders, but a reminder does **not** publish a post. Plans stay on the device.

PostLens 2 also preserves existing saved work and Supporter product identifiers. Improving the workspace should let people continue what they have already started.

## What the Instagram approval means

The photo studio has launched. The Instagram integration has not.

Instagram account connection and direct publishing remain unavailable in the production app while we await Meta approval. There is no publishing date to announce yet. Approval is one requirement; the integration also needs its own release verification before it can become available.

For now, the supported path is straightforward:

1. Select and edit your photo in PostLens.
2. Prepare the composition and caption.
3. Export the result.
4. Open your chosen destination and publish it yourself.

That keeps the useful part of the product available while the separate approval process continues. An export format for Instagram does not mean that PostLens can publish to an Instagram account.
