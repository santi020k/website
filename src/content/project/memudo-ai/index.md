## Designing the path from property discovery to closing

MeMudo.ai is an AI-powered real estate discovery and transaction platform. The product lets clients tune a property feed through likes, dislikes, and stronger match signals while giving owners, brokers, and operations teams the workflows needed to move from interest to a completed sale or rental.

My work covered the product architecture and full-stack foundation: public discovery, role-specific dashboards, API contracts, shared domain rules, brand systems, and the deployment shape for each application.

### Goals

- **Make property discovery adaptive** instead of reducing every search to a static filter form.
- **Model the whole transaction** including visits, negotiation, contracts, insurance, validation, and closing support.
- **Support different service relationships** for broker-led, owner-assisted, and client-found deals.
- **Give every role a focused workspace** without fragmenting the underlying product model.

### What I built

- **A public Next.js experience** for product storytelling, property discovery, and client-facing flows.
- **Role-specific applications** for brokers, administrators, and operations teams.
- **A Hono API on Cloudflare Workers** with shared authentication contracts and an OpenAPI surface.
- **A documentation app and presentation system** for product, architecture, API, philosophy, and investor material.
- **Shared packages** for brand assets, UI primitives, domain logic, and TypeScript contracts.

### Technical highlights

- **Application platform:** `Next.js`, `React`, `TypeScript`, `Tailwind CSS v4`, and OpenNext on Cloudflare Workers.
- **API platform:** `Hono`, typed request contracts, `OpenAPI`, and Swagger documentation.
- **Domain design:** first-class sale and rental intents with `full_service`, `owner_assisted`, and `client_found` transaction paths.
- **Monorepo architecture:** seven app surfaces and shared packages orchestrated through `pnpm` and `Turborepo`.

### Results

- **A product model wider than listings** that connects discovery signals to real transaction services.
- **Clear operational boundaries** for clients, brokers, administrators, and back-office work.
- **A shared foundation** where brand, UI, contracts, and domain decisions move together across the platform.

### Why it matters

Real estate products often optimize the top of the funnel and leave the hard middle disconnected. MeMudo.ai treats matching as the beginning of the relationship, then models the work required to reach a trustworthy close.
