## Co-founding a boutique product studio

At [Justbit.site](https://justbit.site), I learned what it means to own both the product and the business side of delivery. As CTO and co-founder, I worked directly with clients, scoped projects, led implementation, and helped turn ideas into launched products.

## What we shipped

- Custom websites and platforms for startups and small businesses.
- SEO-conscious WordPress and WooCommerce builds for clients that cared about growth as much as design.
- Bespoke React and Node.js products where off-the-shelf solutions were not enough.
- Delivery workflows that balanced speed, quality, and direct collaboration with clients.

## Results

- Launched 10+ client projects in the first year.
- Helped some clients grow organic traffic by as much as 70% through better technical foundations and SEO-focused delivery.
- Built the kind of end-to-end ownership muscle that only shows up when you have to ship, support, and explain the work yourself.

## Why it mattered

Justbit was a crash course in entrepreneurship, communication, and technical ownership. It taught me how to connect user needs, business goals, and engineering decisions without hiding behind process.
