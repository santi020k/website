- 2-sentence summary focused on scope and business context
- 3 bullets max
- 1 strong measurable result in the first bullet
- 1 architecture or systems bullet
- 1 collaboration or leadership bullet

That format will make the profile feel more intentional than the current longer, repetitive entries.
