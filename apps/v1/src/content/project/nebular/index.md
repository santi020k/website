## Exploring Full-Stack and Cross-Platform Development in a High-Growth Tech Studio

From **January 2014 to April 2017**, I worked as a **Full Stack Developer** at [Nebular Technologies](https://nebular.co), a fast-growing software studio specializing in custom-built web and mobile solutions. As one of the company’s early engineers, I had the opportunity to explore the full breadth of development across backend, frontend, and mobile applications in a highly collaborative and mentorship-driven environment.

### 💻 Web, Mobile & Hybrid Experience

Nebular provided a rich, hands-on environment for learning and executing projects in real-world scenarios. I contributed to web and Android apps for clients like **Cosmovisión**, while also working on internal products like **Zeepod** and **Odin**—touching every layer from database models to frontend views.

#### 🚀 Key Results Included

* 🧩 **Built and maintained hybrid apps** using **Cordova** and **AngularJS**, enabling cross-platform deployment for mobile clients.
* 📱 Developed native Android apps in **Java**, learning Android SDK principles and contributing to production releases with stable performance and clean UI/UX.
* 💎 Contributed to web platforms using **Ruby on Rails**, gaining a deep understanding of MVC patterns, ActiveRecord, and Rails conventions.
* 🧪 First exposure to **unit testing** and CI workflows, setting the foundation for a test-driven mindset.
* 🌐 Delivered client-facing websites using **PHP**, **WordPress**, and **WooCommerce**, deploying to environments like **Heroku** and managing database configurations in **PostgreSQL** and **MySQL**.

### 🧠 Business & Platform Context

Nebular focused on helping businesses improve their digital operations through tailor-made web and mobile products. Their value proposition was centered on:

* **Custom Solutions**: Each product was designed to address a specific business need—ranging from internal tools to customer-facing apps.
* **UI/UX Design**: There was a strong emphasis on intuitive interfaces and elegant user journeys, often co-developed with in-house design teams.
* **Agile Collaboration**: We worked in short cycles, often directly with stakeholders, which enabled fast feedback loops and iterative delivery.

---

Working at Nebular gave me my first in-depth exposure to the realities of software engineering—from writing production code and debugging mobile apps to shipping full-stack features and collaborating with designers. It was a formative experience that grounded me in fundamentals while expanding my technical horizons.
