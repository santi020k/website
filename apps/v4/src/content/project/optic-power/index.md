## Shipping across gaming, SaaS, and product engineering work

At [Optic Power](https://www.codepwr.com/), I worked as a senior full stack engineer across several products at once. The throughline was consistent: build dependable software for teams that needed product momentum, technical depth, and steady delivery in a fully remote environment.

### Core challenge

The hard part was not just writing code across several stacks. It was keeping quality and momentum intact while context-switching between client products with different business models, technical constraints, and delivery expectations. The work had to stay adaptable without becoming fragmented.

### What I owned

- **End-to-end product engineering** across architecture, implementation, testing, deployment, and monitoring for multiple clients in parallel.
- **Frontend and backend delivery** spanning `React.js`, `Next.js`, `Node.js`, `GraphQL`, `PostgreSQL`, and `React Native`.
- **Performance and maintainability work** around APIs, query bottlenecks, UI modularization, and state management.
- **Cross-functional collaboration** with product, design, and distributed engineering teams shipping in agile sprints.

### Key outcomes

- **40% better API performance** after refactoring bottlenecks and improving database-query efficiency.
- **Faster front-end experiences** through lazy loading, modularization, and cleaner state normalization.
- **Multiple production launches in parallel** without losing sight of testing, reliability, or developer experience.

### Project spotlight

- **Team Liquid:** contributed to fan rewards and engagement systems tied to streams, events, and social activity, including YouTube and Twitch-related integrations.
- **NurtureBoss:** worked on automated communication flows, third-party integrations, and the infrastructure behind email and SMS delivery.
- **Stardust.gg:** helped build an OAuth-focused React component library and onboarding flows for gaming and Web3 products.

### Tech stack

- **Frontend:** `React.js`, `Next.js`, `Redux`, `Redux Toolkit`, `Redux Sagas`, `Styled Components`, `TailwindCSS`
- **Backend and data:** `Node.js`, `Express.js`, `GraphQL`, `MongoDB`, `PostgreSQL`, `Elixir`, `Phoenix`
- **Mobile and delivery:** `React Native`, `Docker`, `AWS`, `GCP`, `CI/CD`, `Jest`, `Vitest`, `Sentry`

### Why it mattered

Optic Power sharpened my full-stack range. It pushed me to move comfortably between front-end polish, backend performance, third-party integrations, and architecture while staying grounded in what each product actually needed.
